[RECIPE]
ID: B01
Category: Breakfast
Name: Porridge with Fruit Topping
Time: 10 minutes
Calories: ~290 per serving
Ingredients:
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1 cup water
- 1 pinch salt
- 1/2 cup milk (low-fat or skim)
- 1/2 cup mixed fruit (berries, banana slices, diced apple, or canned fruit in juice)
Instructions:
1. Place the oats, water, and salt in a small saucepan. Stir once so the oats are fully wet.
2. Let the mixture sit for about 2 minutes if you have time. This helps the oats soften faster.
3. Put the saucepan on the stove over medium heat.
4. Stir every 30 seconds so the oats do not stick to the bottom of the pan.
5. As soon as the mixture starts bubbling, lower the heat to low.
6. Continue cooking for 3 to 5 minutes, stirring often, until the porridge looks thick and creamy.
7. Turn off the heat. If the porridge looks too thick, add 1 or 2 tablespoons of extra milk and stir.
8. Spoon the porridge into a bowl.
9. Add the fruit on top.
10. Pour the milk over the porridge or serve it on the side.

[RECIPE]
ID: B02
Category: Breakfast
Name: Ham, Cheese, and Spinach Omelette
Time: 10 minutes
Calories: ~330 per serving
Ingredients:
- 2 large eggs
- 1 tablespoon water
- 1 teaspoon soft margarine or butter
- 1/2 cup baby spinach
- 50 g lean ham, chopped
- 2 tablespoons grated parmesan or reduced-fat cheese
- 1 slice whole-grain bread, toasted
- Black pepper to taste
Instructions:
1. Crack the eggs into a bowl. Add the water and black pepper.
2. Whisk with a fork until the yolks and whites are fully mixed and slightly frothy.
3. Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium heat for about 30 seconds.
4. Add the margarine or butter and swirl it around so it lightly coats the pan.
5. Pour in the egg mixture and immediately reduce the heat to low.
6. Let the eggs cook without stirring for about 1 minute, until the bottom starts to set.
7. Sprinkle the cheese over the omelette.
8. Put the spinach and chopped ham on one half of the omelette.
9. Cook for another 1 minute until the top is almost set but still a little soft.
10. Slide a spatula under the empty half and fold it over the filling.
11. Let it cook 30 to 60 seconds more so the spinach softens and the cheese melts.
12. Carefully slide the omelette onto a plate.
13. Serve with the toast on the side.

[RECIPE]
ID: B03
Category: Breakfast
Name: Mango Berry Breakfast Smoothie
Time: 5 minutes
Calories: ~255 per serving
Ingredients:
- 1/2 small mango, peeled and chopped
- 1/2 small banana
- 1/4 cup frozen berries
- 1/2 cup chopped strawberries
- 3/4 cup low-fat milk
- 1/4 cup low-fat natural yogurt
- 1 teaspoon honey
- 1 teaspoon wheat germ or chia seeds (optional)
- 1/4 cup ice
Instructions:
1. Wash the strawberries and cut off the green tops.
2. Peel the mango and banana, then cut them into smaller pieces so they blend easily.
3. Put the mango, banana, berries, strawberries, milk, yogurt, honey, wheat germ or chia seeds, and ice into a blender.
4. Close the lid tightly before blending.
5. Blend on high speed for 30 to 45 seconds.
6. Stop and check the texture. If it is too thick, add a splash of milk. If it is too thin, add a few more frozen berries.
7. Blend again for 10 to 15 seconds until smooth.
8. Pour into a glass and serve right away while cold.

[RECIPE]
ID: B04
Category: Breakfast
Name: Scrambled Eggs on Toast with Tomato
Time: 10 minutes
Calories: ~300 per serving
Ingredients:
- 2 medium eggs
- 2 tablespoons low-fat milk
- 1 teaspoon olive oil
- 1 slice whole-grain bread
- 1 medium tomato
- Black pepper to taste
- 1 teaspoon chopped parsley (optional)
Instructions:
1. Crack the eggs into a bowl. Add the milk, pepper, and parsley if using.
2. Beat with a fork until the mixture is completely smooth and one pale yellow color.
3. Heat the olive oil in a small non-stick pan over medium-low heat.
4. Pour the egg mixture into the pan.
5. Wait 10 seconds, then gently push the eggs from the edges toward the center with a spatula.
6. Keep stirring slowly. Do not rush, or the eggs can become dry.
7. When the eggs look soft and slightly creamy, turn off the heat. They will continue cooking for a few seconds from the pan’s heat.
8. Toast the bread.
9. Slice the tomato in half and either serve fresh or cook it cut-side down in the same pan for 1 minute.
10. Put the scrambled eggs on the toast and serve with the tomato.

[RECIPE]
ID: B05
Category: Breakfast
Name: Bircher Muesli
Time: 10 minutes plus chilling if desired
Calories: ~285 per serving
Ingredients:
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/2 large apple, grated with skin
- 1 teaspoon sunflower seeds
- 1 teaspoon chopped almonds
- 1 teaspoon chopped walnuts
- 1/2 teaspoon ground flaxseed
- 1/2 cup fat-free or low-fat natural yogurt
- 1 tablespoon raisins or sultanas
- 1 tablespoon dried cranberries
- 1 pinch ground cinnamon
Instructions:
1. Wash the apple well. Grate it using the large holes of a grater. There is no need to peel it.
2. Put the oats in a bowl or food container.
3. Add the grated apple, sunflower seeds, almonds, walnuts, flaxseed, yogurt, raisins, cranberries, and cinnamon.
4. Stir very well so the oats are coated and the fruit is spread evenly through the mixture.
5. Taste and add a tiny splash of milk if you want a softer texture.
6. You can eat it immediately, but for best texture cover it and chill in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes or overnight.
7. Stir again before serving.

[RECIPE]
ID: B06
Category: Breakfast
Name: Wholemeal Banana Oat Pancakes
Time: 20 minutes
Calories: ~320 per serving
Ingredients:
- 1/2 ripe banana
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1 egg
- 1/4 cup milk
- 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon canola oil or cooking spray for the pan
- 1 tablespoon sliced fruit for topping (optional)
Instructions:
1. Put the banana in a bowl and mash it thoroughly with a fork until almost smooth.
2. Add the egg and milk, then whisk until combined.
3. Stir in the oats and cinnamon. Let the batter sit for 2 minutes so the oats absorb some liquid.
4. Heat a non-stick pan over medium-low heat. Lightly grease it with canola oil or cooking spray.
5. Spoon small circles of batter into the pan. Leave space between them.
6. Cook for about 2 minutes, until the edges look firmer and you see small bubbles.
7. Flip each pancake carefully with a spatula.
8. Cook the other side for 1 to 2 minutes until golden and fully cooked.
9. Transfer to a plate and repeat with the remaining batter.
10. Serve warm with sliced fruit on top if desired.

- 1/4 cup chopped spinach

[RECIPE]
ID: L01
Category: Lunch
Name: Bean and Roasted Vegetable Salad
Time: 40 minutes
Calories: ~320 per serving
Ingredients:
- 1/2 red bell pepper, thickly sliced
- 1/2 yellow bell pepper, thickly sliced
- 1 small zucchini, sliced
- 50 g mushrooms, halved
- 1/2 small red onion, cut into wedges
- 1/2 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
- 1 garlic clove, crushed
- 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes
- 1/2 cup green beans, trimmed
- 1 tablespoon pine nuts
- 1/2 cup cooked quinoa, couscous, or rice
- Salt and black pepper to taste
Instructions:
1. Preheat the oven to 390°F (200°C).
2. Put the bell peppers, zucchini, mushrooms, and onion in a baking dish.
3. In a small bowl, mix the olive oil, balsamic vinegar, and garlic.
4. Pour this mixture over the vegetables and toss until they are lightly coated.
5. Roast for 20 minutes.
6. Remove the dish carefully, add the cherry tomatoes, and roast for another 10 to 15 minutes until the vegetables are tender.
7. While they roast, bring a small pot of water to a boil.
8. Add the green beans and cook for 3 minutes until bright green and just tender.
9. Drain the beans and rinse quickly under cold water to stop them cooking further.
10. Put the roasted vegetables and green beans into a bowl.
11. Add the cooked quinoa, couscous, or rice.
12. Sprinkle with pine nuts, season with salt and pepper, and serve.

[RECIPE]
ID: L02
Category: Lunch
Name: Honey Soy Beef with Sweet Potato
Time: 30 minutes plus marinating
Calories: ~420 per serving
Ingredients:
- 1 lean beef steak (about 125 g)
- 1 tablespoon low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon mirin or rice vinegar
- 1/2 teaspoon honey
- 1 medium sweet potato, peeled and chopped
- 1 teaspoon olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon grated ginger
- Pinch of finely chopped chili (optional)
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh coriander
- 1 cup steamed greens
Instructions:
1. In a shallow bowl, mix the soy sauce, mirin, and honey.
2. Add the beef and turn it so both sides are coated.
3. Cover and let it marinate in the refrigerator for 20 to 30 minutes if possible.
4. Meanwhile, put the sweet potato into a pot of water and bring it to a boil.
5. Cook for about 12 minutes or until very soft when pierced with a fork.
6. Drain the sweet potato and mash it with a fork.
7. Heat the olive oil in a small pan, then add the ginger and chili if using.
8. Cook for 30 seconds, then mix it into the sweet potato along with the coriander.
9. Heat a grill pan or frying pan over medium-high heat.
10. Cook the beef for about 2 to 3 minutes per side, or longer if you prefer it more done.
11. Let the beef rest on a plate for 2 minutes before slicing.
12. Serve the sliced beef with the mashed sweet potato and steamed greens.

[RECIPE]
ID: L15
Category: Lunch
Name: Quick Lentil Soup
Time: 40 minutes
Calories: ~330 per serving
Ingredients:
- 1/2 tablespoon olive oil
- 1/2 onion, chopped
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 small carrot, chopped
- 1 tomato, chopped
- 1 teaspoon tomato paste
- 3/4 cup dried lentils, rinsed
- 3 cups water or low-sodium broth
- 1 bay leaf
- Salt and black pepper to taste
Instructions:
1. Heat the olive oil in a pot over medium heat.
2. Add the onion, garlic, and carrot.
3. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring often, until the onion softens.
4. Add the chopped tomato and tomato paste and stir.
5. Cook for 3 minutes.
6. Add the lentils, water or broth, and bay leaf.
7. Bring to a boil.
8. Lower the heat, cover loosely, and simmer for 25 to 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
9. Add more water if the soup becomes too thick before the lentils are soft.
10. Remove the bay leaf.
11. Season with salt and pepper and serve.
